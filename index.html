추가 편성다른 홈쇼핑사 입점라인 확장
협업 방식

필요한 만큼, 또는 처음부터 끝까지.

  • 운영

    제품이 준비돼 있을 때. 입점, 심의, 방송 운영을 맡습니다.

  • 기획

    방송용 상품 컨셉과 구성만 필요할 때.

  • 기획 + 운영

    상품 기획부터 방송 운영까지.

  • 브랜드 파트너

    위의 전부에 브랜드 이름 · 디자인, 라인 확장, 방송 이후 성장까지. 1위 사례 세 건 모두 이 방식이었습니다.

진행 순서

첫 미팅부터 추가 편성까지.

  1. 상담제품과 지금 상황을 듣습니다.
  2. 진단맞는 홈쇼핑사와 카테고리를 제안합니다.
  3. 상품 설계컨셉, 구성, 가격을 정합니다.
  4. 방송 준비심의, 영상, 판넬, 편성.
  5. 론칭생방송을 함께 운영합니다.
  6. 추가 편성결과를 근거로 다음 방송을 잡습니다.

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Can I apply with a small following?

There is no minimum follower count. We look at your channel's voice, how consistently you post, and how well you know your field.

Do I have to sign exclusively?

No. DEF does not require exclusivity. You can work with DEF and with others at the same time.

Can I turn down an offer?

Yes. If it is not a fit for you, just tell us so.

How are fees and payment dates decided?

We put how it is calculated, what is deducted and when it is paid in writing before you sign.

Can I keep my existing brand partnerships?

Yes. Because DEF does not require exclusivity, you do not have to end anything you already have.

How far can my content be used?

Paid usage, re-edits, duration, territory and likeness are agreed for each project in advance. Nothing is used beyond what was agreed.

How does a partnership end?

The term and how to end it are written into the contract. If work is in progress when it ends, we agree together how to finish it.

Can I apply from outside Korea?

Yes. We will confirm which regions and languages we can work in, and how payments are handled.
